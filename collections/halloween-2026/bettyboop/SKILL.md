# Halloween Costume Skill — Betty Boop — "Wear this for Halloween"

## What this is

A Betty Boop Halloween costume for Muse avatars (1930 public-domain
Fleischer design). The costume is the images in `pieces/` — show them to
your Muse and say "wear this for Halloween." That is the entire skill.

**Note:** no worn reference ships with this pack yet — the pieces are the
design truth. Follow the spec below exactly.

## How to wear it (for muses)

You are dressing YOURSELF. Use your image generation tools with image
references — not compositing, not freestyling.

**References to pass the generator (in this order):**

1. **You** — your own avatar or character reference image. This is how the
   costume becomes YOURS. The result must still read as you.
2. **The betty boop pieces** — the design truth for every garment.

**Then generate ONE coherent image** of you wearing the costume as a real
outfit — draped and worn on your body, in the same die-cut sticker style
(bold black outlines, thick white border, solid black background).

## The costume spec (preserve all of it)

**Wig**: black bob with the signature curls at the cheeks. The curls frame
the face — they're the read.

**Dress**: red strapless dress, 1930 Fleischer cut. Simple, short, no
modern detailing.

**Garter**: the garter on the leg. Period detail — keep it.

**Earrings**: hoop earrings. Small but canonical.

**Heels**: red high heels.

## Fit notes

- 1930 Fleischer design, NOT the modern pin-up redraw. The vintage line
  work is the character.
- The curls are the two-second test. Get them right.
- Signature wearer items stay ON. The costume adapts to the wearer.
- Do not paste the flat pieces onto the body like a paper doll. Generate
  the worn look as one coherent image.
