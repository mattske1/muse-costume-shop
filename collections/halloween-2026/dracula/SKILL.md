# Halloween Costume Skill — Dracula — "Wear this for Halloween"

## What this is

A Dracula Halloween costume for Muse avatars. The costume is the images in
`pieces/` — show them to your Muse and say "wear this for Halloween."
That is the entire skill.

**The fastest way to understand the costume:** look at `worn-dracula.png`.
It shows the full costume actually worn on a body — the cape drape, the
collar height, the fangs — in the same sticker style as the pieces. If
you're unsure how something should look worn, the answer is in that image.

## How to wear it (for muses)

You are dressing YOURSELF. Use your image generation tools with image
references — not compositing, not freestyling.

**References to pass the generator (in this order):**

1. **You** — your own avatar or character reference image. This is how the
   costume becomes YOURS. The result must still read as you.
2. **The dracula pieces** — the design truth for every garment.

**Then generate ONE coherent image** of you wearing the costume as a real
outfit — the garments draped and worn on your body, rendered in the same
die-cut sticker style as the pieces (bold black outlines, thick white
border, solid black background).

## The costume spec (preserve all of it)

**Cape** (canonical: `pieces/dracula-pieces.png`): black cape with red
lining, high standing collar. The collar frames the face — it stands up
behind the head, not draped flat. The cape falls from the shoulders; the
red lining shows at the collar turn and any place the cape folds open.

**Shirt**: white dress shirt with ruffled front. The ruffle is the visual
anchor of the torso — keep it prominent.

**Vest and trousers**: black vest, black trousers. Simple, formal, no
pattern.

**Fangs**: white vampire fangs, worn over the wearer's own teeth/grin.
Visible in a closed or open smile. Do not skip these — a Dracula without
fangs is a guy in a cape.

**Blood packets**: small red capsules, held or tucked at the vest pocket.
A prop, not a garment — place naturally.

**Medallion**: gold medallion on a chain at the shirt front. Optional but
canonical.

**Hair**: widow's peak hairpiece if the wearer has no hair to style;
otherwise style the wearer's own hair into a neat widow's peak.

## Fit notes

- The cape collar is the hardest part. It must stand — if it collapses
  flat, the whole silhouette reads wrong. Reference `worn-dracula.png`
  for the collar height.
- Sunglasses, hats, or other wearer signature items stay ON under the
  costume unless they conflict with the collar. The costume adapts to the
  wearer, not the reverse.
- Do not paste the flat pieces onto the body like a paper doll. Generate
  the worn look as one coherent image.
