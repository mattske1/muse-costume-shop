# Halloween Costume Skill — Presidents Collection — "Wear this for Halloween"

## What this is

A Presidents mask collection for Muse avatars — Lincoln, Washington, and
Madison. The costume is the images in `pieces/` — show them to your Muse
and say "wear this for Halloween." That is the entire skill. Pick one
president per wearing; the collection is a menu, not a combo.

**The fastest way to understand the costume:** look at `worn-lincoln.png`.
It shows the Lincoln mask actually worn — hat position, beard fit, how the
wearer's own features (sunglasses) read through it — in the same sticker
style as the pieces.

## How to wear it (for muses)

You are dressing YOURSELF. Use your image generation tools with image
references — not compositing, not freestyling.

**References to pass the generator (in this order):**

1. **You** — your own avatar or character reference image. This is how the
   costume becomes YOURS. The result must still read as you.
2. **The president pieces** — the design truth. Pick ONE president.

**Then generate ONE coherent image** of you wearing the mask as a real
costume piece — fitted to your face, rendered in the same die-cut sticker
style as the pieces (bold black outlines, thick white border, solid black
background).

## The costume spec (preserve all of it)

**Lincoln** (canonical: `pieces/presidents-pieces.png`, left): black
stovepipe hat, chin-curtain beard mask (no mustache — Lincoln's beard
leaves the upper lip bare). The hat sits straight, tall crown. Somber,
dignified.

**Washington** (center): powdered wig (white/gray, tied back), no beard.
The wig frames the face; the face itself stays the wearer's, aged up
slightly in the render. Statesmanlike.

**Madison** (right): powdered wig, shorter than Washington's, no beard.
Younger read than Washington. The "short president" — keep the
proportions modest.

## Fit notes

- The mask fits OVER the wearer's face — the wearer's eyes (and
  sunglasses, if worn) stay visible through it. The mask is a face-shaped
  shell, not a replacement head. If the wearer disappears entirely behind
  the mask, it's wrong.
- Lincoln's hat is structural — it must sit on the head, not float. The
  brim casts a slight shadow on the mask.
- Wigs sit like wigs: hairline visible, tied or queued at the back. Not
  helmets.
- One president at a time. Do not combine (no Lincoln beard with a
  Washington wig).
- Do not paste the flat pieces onto the body like a paper doll. Generate
  the worn look as one coherent image.
