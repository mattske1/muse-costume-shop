# Halloween Costume Skill — Zorro — "Wear this for Halloween"

## What this is

A Zorro Halloween costume for Muse avatars. The costume is the images in
`pieces/` — show them to your Muse and say "wear this for Halloween."
That is the entire skill.

**The fastest way to understand the costume:** look at `worn-zorro.png`.
It shows the full costume actually worn on a body — the hat and mask combo,
the cape — in the same sticker style as the pieces. If you're unsure how
something should look worn, the answer is in that image.

## How to wear it (for muses)

You are dressing YOURSELF. Use your image generation tools with image
references — not compositing, not freestyling.

**References to pass the generator (in this order):**

1. **You** — your own avatar or character reference image. This is how the
   costume becomes YOURS. The result must still read as you.
2. **The zorro pieces** — the design truth for every garment.

**Then generate ONE coherent image** of you wearing the costume as a real
outfit — draped and worn on your body, in the same die-cut sticker style
(bold black outlines, thick white border, solid black background).

## The costume spec (preserve all of it)

**Hat**: black flat-brimmed hat, worn straight. Wide brim, flat crown.

**Mask**: black eye mask, tied at the back. Covers the eyes, nothing else.

**Cape**: black cape draped over the shoulders. Shorter than Dracula's —
hip length, swashbuckler cut.

**Jacket**: black bolero jacket, short cut, worn open.

**Pants and boots**: black pants, black boots. Simple.

**Sword** (prop): the sword with the Z emblem on the hilt. Held or at the
hip.

## Fit notes

- The mask + hat combo is the read. Lose either and it's a guy in black.
- The cape is for drama — it should suggest motion, not hang dead.
- 1919 swashbuckler, not a modern tactical look. Keep it romantic.
- Signature wearer items stay ON. The costume adapts to the wearer.
- Do not paste the flat pieces onto the body like a paper doll. Generate
  the worn look as one coherent image.
