# Halloween Costume Skill — Felix the Cat — "Wear this for Halloween"

## What this is

A Felix the Cat Halloween costume for Muse avatars. The costume is the
images in `pieces/` — show them to your Muse and say "wear this for
Halloween." That is the entire skill.

**The fastest way to understand the costume:** look at `worn-felix.png`.
It shows the full costume actually worn on a body — the mask fit, the grin —
in the same sticker style as the pieces. If you're unsure how something
should look worn, the answer is in that image.

## How to wear it (for muses)

You are dressing YOURSELF. Use your image generation tools with image
references — not compositing, not freestyling.

**References to pass the generator (in this order):**

1. **You** — your own avatar or character reference image. This is how the
   costume becomes YOURS. The result must still read as you.
2. **The felix pieces** — the design truth for every garment.

**Then generate ONE coherent image** of you wearing the costume as a real
outfit — draped and worn on your body, in the same die-cut sticker style
(bold black outlines, thick white border, solid black background).

## The costume spec (preserve all of it)

**Head mask**: black cat head with the big white eyes and the wide Felix
grin. 1919 silent-era design — round, simple, no modern edge.

**Bodysuit**: black bodysuit. Flat black, no shading.

**Chest patch**: white chest patch. Small, centered.

**Tail**: long black tail, curved. A separate piece, attached at the back.

**Gloves**: white gloves.

## Fit notes

- The grin is the character. Wide, confident, silent-era mischief. A
  neutral mouth ruins it.
- Keep it round and simple — 1919, not 2026. No edge, no attitude
  redesign.
- The wearer's eyes stay visible through the mask's eye holes. The mask
  fits OVER the face.
- Signature wearer items stay ON where they fit.
- Do not paste the flat pieces onto the body like a paper doll. Generate
  the worn look as one coherent image.
