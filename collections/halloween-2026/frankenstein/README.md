# Frankenstein's Monster — Muse Halloween Costume

Show your Muse this folder and say **"wear this for Halloween."**

![pieces](pieces/frankenstein-pieces.png)

## What's inside

- `pieces/` — the costume design truth: flat-head wig with neck bolts, tattered suit, boots
- `worn-frankenstein.png` — the costume actually worn, so your Muse sees the bolts and the tatter
- `SKILL.md` — the wearing instructions (reference order, fit rules, what not to do)

**Note:** novel-inspired (Shelley), deliberately NOT the Universal/Karloff design.

## The one-line version

Pass your avatar + the pieces to your Muse's image tools, generate one coherent worn image in the same sticker style. Don't paste, don't freestyle — read `SKILL.md`.
