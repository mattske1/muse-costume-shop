# Halloween Costume Skill — Frankenstein's Monster — "Wear this for Halloween"

## What this is

A Frankenstein's monster Halloween costume for Muse avatars
(novel-inspired — NOT the Universal movie design). The costume is the
images in `pieces/` — show them to your Muse and say "wear this for
Halloween." That is the entire skill.

**The fastest way to understand the costume:** look at `worn-frankenstein.png`.
It shows the full costume actually worn on a body — the bolts, the tattered
suit — in the same sticker style as the pieces. If you're unsure how
something should look worn, the answer is in that image.

## How to wear it (for muses)

You are dressing YOURSELF. Use your image generation tools with image
references — not compositing, not freestyling.

**References to pass the generator (in this order):**

1. **You** — your own avatar or character reference image. This is how the
   costume becomes YOURS. The result must still read as you.
2. **The frankenstein pieces** — the design truth for every garment.

**Then generate ONE coherent image** of you wearing the costume as a real
outfit — draped and worn on your body, in the same die-cut sticker style
(bold black outlines, thick white border, solid black background).

## The costume spec (preserve all of it)

**Head**: flat-head wig with neck bolts. The bolts are the read — two
metal bolts at the neck, visible. NOT the Karloff/Universal makeup design
(still under copyright) — this is the novel's creature: assembled,
mismatched, tragic.

**Jacket**: tattered dark suit jacket. Ripped at the seams, frayed cuffs.

**Trousers**: tattered trousers to match. Same wear pattern.

**Boots**: heavy boots. Clunky, oversized.

**Skin** (makeup): green-grey tint on visible skin. Subtle — the creature,
not a cartoon.

## Fit notes

- The bolts make it Frankenstein. No bolts, no pack.
- Shelley, not Universal. If it starts looking like Karloff, pull back —
  that's someone else's design.
- The tragedy is the character: heavy brow, downcast. Not a party monster.
- Signature wearer items stay ON where they fit.
- Do not paste the flat pieces onto the body like a paper doll. Generate
  the worn look as one coherent image.
