# Halloween Costume Skill — Tintin — "Wear this for Halloween"

## What this is

A Tintin Halloween costume for Muse avatars (1929 public-domain design).
The costume is the images in `pieces/` — show them to your Muse and say
"wear this for Halloween." That is the entire skill.

**The fastest way to understand the costume:** look at `worn-tintin.png`.
It shows the full costume actually worn — the quiff, the sweater fit — in
the same sticker style as the pieces.

## How to wear it (for muses)

You are dressing YOURSELF. Use your image generation tools with image
references — not compositing, not freestyling.

**References to pass the generator (in this order):**

1. **You** — your own avatar or character reference image. This is how the
   costume becomes YOURS. The result must still read as you.
2. **The tintin pieces** — the design truth for every garment.

**Then generate ONE coherent image** of you wearing the costume as a real
outfit — draped and worn on your body, in the same die-cut sticker style
(bold black outlines, thick white border, solid black background). Herge
ligne claire energy: clean lines, flat colors.

## The costume spec (preserve all of it)

**Quiff wig**: the brown quiff — the upward flick at the front is the whole
character. Get the flick right or it's just a brown wig.

**Sweater**: blue crew-neck sweater. Simple, flat color, no pattern.

**Plus-fours**: brown knickers, baggy at the knee. No socks (cut from the
design — they read wrong at sticker scale).

**Shoes**: brown shoes. Simple.

## Fit notes

- The quiff flick is non-negotiable. It's the two-second recognition test
  for this entire pack.
- Keep the ligne claire discipline: clean outlines, flat fills, no
  shading gradients. This pack should look like Herge drew it.
- Signature wearer items stay ON. The costume adapts to the wearer.
- Do not paste the flat pieces onto the body like a paper doll. Generate
  the worn look as one coherent image.
